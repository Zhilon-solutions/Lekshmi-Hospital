Health & Wellness Tips

Simple steps to maintain good health and prevent common illnesses

Stay Hydrated

Drink at least 8-10 glasses of water daily. Proper hydration helps maintain body temperature, aids digestion, and keeps your skin healthy.

Quality Sleep

Get 7-8 hours of sleep each night. Good sleep strengthens immunity, improves concentration, and helps your body recover and repair.

Balanced Diet

Include fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and proteins in your meals. A balanced diet provides essential nutrients for optimal health.

Regular Exercise

Engage in 30 minutes of physical activity daily. Walking, jogging, or yoga helps maintain healthy weight and reduces disease risk.

Regular Check-ups

Schedule routine health screenings. Early detection of health issues leads to better treatment outcomes and prevents complications.

Hand Hygiene

Wash hands frequently with soap and water. Good hand hygiene is the simplest way to prevent infections and stay healthy.
